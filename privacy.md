WNDR Privacy Policy
Last updated: November 6, 2026 Developer: Alex Contact: al_man@hotmail.com

What WNDR does
WNDR (listed on the App Store as WANDERLST) shows Wikipedia articles about places near your location.

Location
WNDR uses your device location while the app is open to find nearby places.
Your location is used on your device and to send search requests. It is not stored on our servers, because WNDR has no servers.
The app asks for "When In Use" access only. It does not track you in the background.
You can use a map pin to explore any area without sharing your real location.
Data sent to third parties
When you use WNDR, the app sends requests to:

Wikipedia / Wikimedia Foundation (search and article text and images). See their privacy policy: https://foundation.wikimedia.org/wiki/Policy:Privacy_policy
Wikidata (food suggestions, using the city name only).
Apple (map and reverse-geocoding services that turn coordinates into place names). See: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/
These requests include your approximate coordinates or place name. We do not add an account or device ID to them.

What we don't do
No accounts or sign-in
No advertising or ad tracking
No analytics or crash reporting that identifies you
We don't sell or share personal data
Stored on your device
Bookmarks and viewing history are kept only while the app is open. They are not saved when you close the app, and no personal data is stored on our servers.

Tips
Optional tips are processed by Apple through the App Store. We don't receive your payment details.

Children
WNDR is not directed at children under 13.

Changes
We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.
