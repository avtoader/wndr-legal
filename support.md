# WNDR Support

WNDR (listed on the App Store as WANDERLST) shows Wikipedia articles about places near you.

## Frequently asked questions

**Why does WNDR need my location?**
It uses your location while the app is open to find Wikipedia articles near you. You can also use the map icon to explore any area without sharing your real location.

**Why are there no articles near me?**
Some areas have few geotagged Wikipedia articles. Try a nearby city with the map icon, or tap Refresh Feed.

**Does WNDR have accounts or ads?**
No. There are no accounts, sign-in, or advertising.

**Where does the content come from?**
Article text comes from Wikipedia and is shared under CC BY-SA 4.0. Each card links to the full article.

**How do I remove bookmarks or history?**
Bookmarks and history are kept only while the app is open. Close the app to clear them.
